# Screenshot-safe quotes

Apple rejected the **listing**, and the most likely trigger was a screenshot
showing a quote that named Larry King. The archive is clean now, but the
structural fix is to stop capturing screenshots from the random feed and use a
fixed set instead. A marketing asset should be chosen, not sampled.

## How these were picked

Filtered from all 6,124 quotes, keeping only those that contain **no proper
nouns at all** — no real people, no brands, no shows, no hashtags, no handles.
605 quotes qualified. These are the 24 best of them: the voice is unmistakable,
and there is no third party anywhere in them to object.

Then hand-screened again to drop anything awkward on a store page: nothing
political, nothing about drink, vapes or nitrous, nothing suggestive, nothing
touching the marriages gag.

## Using them

Pick six to ten, one per screenshot, and keep this list for every future
capture. If the app shows a random quote on launch, add a temporary debug way to
pin a specific quote for captures rather than relaunching until the right one
appears — relaunching until a safe quote shows is exactly the lottery that got
the app rejected.

Nothing here needs a disclaimer, an attribution or a rights check, which is the
whole point.
